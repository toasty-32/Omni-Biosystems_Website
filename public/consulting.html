The honest version

Most chip migrations fail on the parts nobody budgets for.

Not the biology. The hydrogel that will not load reproducibly. The geometry that traps a bubble at hour fourteen. The segmentation model that was trained on someone else's cell line. We have hit all of these, and we would rather you did not.

Services

Four places we add value.

Assay migration

We take an existing well-plate or Transwell assay and re-express it in a chip format — preserving the endpoint, gaining the microenvironment.

Chip design & fabrication

Compartment geometry, micropillar barriers, and fluidic routing — then injection-molded at production volumes, not lab-bench one-offs.

Automation strategy

Where to automate first, what to leave manual, and what throughput is actually achievable within your budget. Frequently our advice is to automate less than you planned.

AI analysis pipelines

Segmentation and tracking models trained on your imagery, validated against your manual scoring, delivered as a pipeline your team can run.

Engagement model

Small, cheap, reversible — before big and expensive.
